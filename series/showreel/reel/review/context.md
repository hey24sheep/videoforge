A 15-second 16:9 showreel for an open-source AI video tool, meant as the hero image of its GitHub page. Intended look:
photoreal Hollywood science-fiction, cold blue shadows and warm gold light, slow camera moves, light film grain. Four
shots (a lone astronaut on a frozen sea under a giant planet; an empty gold-visor helmet on ice; a ring space station
over a planet; boot prints leading to a black monolith with a beam of light) and a title card. A small "videoforge"
watermark in the bottom-right corner of every shot is intended. The sheet shows 2 frames per second, left to right,
top to bottom.
