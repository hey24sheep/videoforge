A 15-second 16:9 showreel for an open-source AI video tool, meant as the hero image of its GitHub page. Intended look:
photoreal Hollywood science-fiction, cold blue shadows and warm gold light, slow camera moves, light film grain. Shots,
about 3 s each: (1) a lone astronaut on a frozen sea at golden hour under a giant ringed planet; (2) an empty helmet
with a gold mirror visor on the ice, reflecting the planet; (3) an ice canyon at night under green and gold aurora;
(4) boot prints leading up a snowy ridge to a black monolith with a beam of light, a small astronaut at its base;
(5) a title card. A small "videoforge" watermark in the bottom-right corner of every frame is intended. The sheet
shows 2 frames per second, left to right, top to bottom.
