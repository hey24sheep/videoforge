mean >= 7 with no criterion < 5 (hook, clarity, suspense, dialogue, interrogation, believability, ending, visual proof).

without a fact sheet scored 5.5-6.6 for six rounds (2026-11-08). A narrated series keeps its narrator's voice-over.
